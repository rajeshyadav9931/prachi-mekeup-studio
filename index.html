Your learning journey starts with a conversation.

Can I enquire if I’m a complete beginner?+

Yes. Our advertised course covers basic to advanced makeup and hair styling. Tell us about your experience so we can discuss the starting point and current course plan with you.

What does the makeup course cover?+

The course syllabus includes skin analysis, colour theory, product knowledge, full-face demonstrations, eye makeup, occasion looks, bridal portfolio, hair styling, saree and dupatta draping, and practice sessions.

What are the fees, duration and batch timings?+

Enquire with the academy for the current fees, course duration, class schedule and next batch. Also ask about kit requirements and certification details before enrolling.

Where is the academy in Ranchi?+

Find Prachi Makeup Studio & Academy on Ratu Road, near Shree Leather, Panchsheel Colony, Pandra, Ranchi 834005. Call +91 62993 85690 to discuss a visit.

Do you also offer bridal and party makeup?+

Yes. Alongside teaching, we welcome bridal and occasion makeup enquiries. Select your service below and share your event date, location and preferred look.
